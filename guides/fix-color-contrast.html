How to fix low color contrast (WCAG 1.4.3)

WCAG AA asks for a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text (24px, or 18.66px bold).

  • Light gray text on white is the most common failure. Darken the text to #595959 or darker on white.
  • Text over photos: add a solid or semi-opaque background behind the text.
  • Check button text and placeholder text too.
