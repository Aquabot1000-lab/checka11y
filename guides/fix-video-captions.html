How to add captions to videos (WCAG 1.2.2)

Pre-recorded videos with audio need synchronized captions. Deaf and hard-of-hearing visitors depend on them, and many other people watch with the sound off.

Steps

  • YouTube: YouTube Studio → Subtitles. Start from the auto-captions, then correct names, numbers and punctuation.
  • Vimeo: Video settings → Distribution → Subtitles. Upload a .vtt or .srt file.
  • Self-hosted: <video><track kind="captions" src="captions.vtt" srclang="en" label="English"></video>

Uncorrected auto-captions often contain errors, so review them before you rely on them. Automated scanners can check whether a caption track exists, but not whether it's accurate.
