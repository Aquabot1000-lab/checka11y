Website accessibility checklist for small businesses

A practical starting list. Items marked (auto) can be found by an automated scan; the rest need a person to check.

  1. Every meaningful image has alt text (auto finds missing ones; a person judges quality).
  2. Text contrast is at least 4.5:1 (auto).
  3. Every form field has a visible label (auto).
  4. Links and buttons have names (auto).
  5. The page language is set (auto).
  6. Headings are in a logical order (partly auto).
  7. Everything works with the keyboard alone. See the keyboard test.
  8. The focus outline is visible.
  9. Videos have accurate captions.
  10. No content flashes more than 3 times per second.
  11. Error messages on forms say what to fix.
  12. Text can zoom to 200% without breaking the layout.
  13. Tap targets are at least 24×24 px (auto: WCAG 2.2 target-size).
  14. PDFs such as menus and forms are accessible, or also available as web pages.
  15. An accessibility contact or statement page tells visitors how to report problems.

This checklist is informational and is not legal advice or a complete WCAG audit.
