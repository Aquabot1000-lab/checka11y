How to make your site keyboard accessible

Automated scanners, including ours, can't fully test keyboard use. This 5-minute manual check covers what they miss.

The 5-minute test

  1. Click the address bar, then press Tab repeatedly.
  2. Can you always see where you are? If not, the focus outline was removed (WCAG 2.4.7).
  3. Can you reach every link, button, menu and form field? Open menus with Enter or Space.
  4. Can you get out of every popup or menu with Esc or Tab? If you get stuck, that's a keyboard trap (WCAG 2.1.2).
  5. Does the order make sense, top to bottom and left to right?

Common fixes

  • Never use outline:none without a replacement. Use :focus-visible{outline:3px solid #f59e0b}.
