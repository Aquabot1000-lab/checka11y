How to fix missing form labels

Placeholder text is not a label: it disappears as soon as you type, and some screen readers don't read it.

  • Use <label for="email">Email</label> paired with id="email".
  • Most form plugins (Gravity Forms, WPForms, Wix Forms) have a "show label" setting. Turn it on.
  • Dropdowns (select) need labels too.
